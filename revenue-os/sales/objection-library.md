# Objection Library

## Too Early

Response direction: propose a fixed-scope review instead of implementation.

## We Can Build Internally

Response direction: position the work as a fast external audit or focused sprint that reduces internal uncertainty.

## Compliance Is Legal's Job

Response direction: clarify that the offer covers engineering workflows, evidence, integrations, and incident operations, not legal advice.

## AI Is Not Reliable Enough

Response direction: agree on the risk and offer evaluation, observability, fallback, and human-review design.
