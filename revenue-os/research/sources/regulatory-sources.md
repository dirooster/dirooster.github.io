# Regulatory Sources

Use official sources before any public CRA or AI Act claim.

## CRA

- EU Digital Strategy, Cyber Resilience Act overview: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cyber-resilience-act
- EUR-Lex Regulation (EU) 2024/2847: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj
- Verified on 2026-08-16: the Commission's CRA overview says the CRA entered into force on 2024-12-10, main obligations apply from 2027-12-11, and reporting obligations apply from 2026-09-11.

Working public wording:

> We implement engineering workflows and evidence infrastructure for CRA readiness. We are not a law firm and do not provide legal compliance opinions.

## AI Act

- European Commission AI Act overview: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai
- EUR-Lex Regulation (EU) 2024/1689: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj
- Verified on 2026-08-16: the Commission's AI Act overview says the AI Act entered into force on 2024-08-01 and became applicable on 2026-08-02 with exceptions, including extended high-risk timelines after the AI Omnibus changes.

Operating rule:

AI Act is secondary research only during the initial sprint. Verify current official guidance before publishing offer claims.
